# Evolve Therapy INC Website Rebuild

## Goal
Build a polished, accessible, locally relevant mental-health website using the selected **Editorial Warmth** direction: warm editorial typography, deep evergreen, clay accents, natural photography, generous whitespace, and restrained motion.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and Homepage
- Establish the global design system with Fraunces and Work Sans from Google Fonts, semantic colors, typography, spacing, buttons, focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Create an accessible header, desktop navigation, functional mobile menu, and reusable footer.
- Build the homepage in the required order:
  1. Jacksonville-focused service and location introduction
  2. Welcome / practice philosophy
  3. Four core service pathways
  4. LGBTQ+ affirming care
  5. Trauma-informed care and EMDR
  6. Clinician preview with owner-verification placeholders
  7. Baymeadows location
  8. Patient FAQs
  9. Appointment/contact call to action
- Generate cohesive editorial photography for the office and supporting care sections. Do not generate fake clinician portraits; those will remain professional placeholders until real photos and verified details are provided.

## Phase 2 — Core Patient Pages
Create reusable service-page layouts and distinct pages for:
- Psychotherapy
- EMDR Therapy
- Trauma Therapy
- Anxiety Therapy
- Depression Therapy
- Grief Counseling
- LGBTQ+ Affirming Therapy
- Psychiatric Medication Management
- Group Therapy
- Telehealth
- Insurance & Payment
- Become a Patient

Each page will answer what the service is, who it may support, what to expect, who provides it, location/availability, and how to get started. Medical claims and unknown details will remain conservative or visibly marked for owner review.

## Phase 3 — Practice, Team, Professionals, and Resources
- Our Practice and Our Team pages, plus a reusable clinician profile layout.
- Separate professional-services area for clinical supervision, EMDR consultation/certification, and internships.
- Resources, FAQs, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and Accessibility pages.
- Only services and programs confirmed by the source material will be presented as available; unresolved offerings will be marked for verification rather than invented.

## Search, Accessibility, and Trust
- Add unique page titles, descriptions, social metadata, semantic headings, useful internal links, image alt text, and local business structured data where facts are verified.
- Keep Evolve Therapy INC and the verified Baymeadows address consistent throughout.
- Add keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, accessible FAQ controls, readable contrast, stable image dimensions, and mobile layouts without horizontal overflow.
- Include crisis guidance that clearly separates emergencies from routine appointment contact.

## Verification and Quality Checks
- Test primary navigation, mobile menu, FAQ behavior, action links, and page rendering on desktop and mobile.
- Review the site for layout overlap, missing content, broken links, accessibility basics, and metadata coverage.
- Maintain an owner-verification checklist for phone, email, hours, appointment URL, telehealth availability, clinicians and credentials, insurance, fees, groups, professional programs, and social profiles.

## Scope Boundary
The implemented site will be a production-quality website experience in this project. External WordPress migration, live-domain changes, directory updates, analytics accounts, Search Console, and verified business data entry require owner access or confirmed information and are not performed without those inputs.
